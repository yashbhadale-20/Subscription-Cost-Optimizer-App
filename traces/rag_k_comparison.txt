[Chunk 8] Cancellation and refunds
Cancellation rules, refund eligibility, and early termination fees can affect the total cost. These terms should be checked in the provider's current policy before making a purchase decision.

[Chunk 3] Discounts and promotions
Promotional prices may apply only for a limited period. A fair comparison should record the promotion duration and the price after the promotion ends. A simple monthly-times-twelve calculation assumes the same price for all twelve months.

[Chunk 4] Taxes and fees
Taxes and service fees can increase the amount paid beyond the advertised subscription price. The basic cost calculator in this prototype does not calculate taxes or extra fees.
